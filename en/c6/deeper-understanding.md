# Deeper understanding — Lecture 06 — Persistence with Sequelize and SQLite

Use these readings after the lecture to revisit its concepts in more depth.

- [SQLite: Datatypes](https://www.sqlite.org/datatype3.html) — understand SQLite storage classes, type affinity, and strict tables.
- [SQLite: Constraints](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_createtable.html#constraints) — see where durable integrity rules are enforced.


- [Sequelize 6: validations and constraints](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/core-concepts/validations-and-constraints/) — revisit built-in and custom validators, null handling, and model-wide rules beside example 02.
- [Sequelize 6: creating in bulk](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/core-concepts/model-querying-basics/#creating-in-bulk) — check validation options before replacing create/save with a bulk method.
- [Sequelize 6: raw queries](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/core-concepts/raw-queries/) — compare replacements with bind parameters; neither parameterizes identifiers.
