# Deeper understanding — Lecture 07 — Relationships, Transactions and API Design

Use these readings after the lecture to revisit its concepts in more depth.

- [SQLite: Transaction language](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_transaction.html) — examine deferred, immediate, and exclusive transaction behavior.
- [Sequelize 6: Constraints and circularities](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/other-topics/constraints-and-circularities/) — explore schema-ordering problems in related models.


- [Sequelize 6 associations](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/core-concepts/assocs/) — locate the foreign key and distinguish source/target navigation.
- [Sequelize eager loading](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/advanced-association-concepts/eager-loading/) — compare optional/required includes, through attributes and child ordering.
- [Sequelize managed transactions](https://sequelize.org/docs/v6/other-topics/transactions/) — trace callback rejection, rollback, commit and the outer returned promise.
- [SQLite foreign keys](https://www.sqlite.org/foreignkeys.html) — review enforcement and delete actions beside example 01.
- [SQLite isolation](https://www.sqlite.org/isolation.html) — distinguish writer serialization from application-level concurrency rules.
